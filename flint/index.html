Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Flint

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is simple maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep vegetation trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and schedule a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still cheap.

Take a look at the age of your system. Its age tells you a lot. With regular service, most air conditioners make it to 12 to 15 years, gas furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is beyond that range and also shows rising bills, uneven temperatures, or repeated repairs, it's a strong candidate for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and less stressful than a rushed emergency install when the first heat wave hits.

The earlier you report a comfort problem, the cheaper it usually is. A capacitor that's starting to fail is an easy, low-cost part to replace, yet the same part, ignored through a full season, can ruin the compressor and force a full system replacement. Save our number in your contacts now so help is one call away as soon as something seems off in your home. Early intervention saves time, money, and sleepless nights.
